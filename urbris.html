Paint by Ride
Every saved route, colored by how it was verified - blue where you personally captured it on camera, green where it's Street View only. Nothing painted means nowhere verified yet.
Coverage gaps
Click a point on the map to scan every road within an area for Street View gaps - a fast way to find where to point a GoPro instead of checking one route at a time.
Compare route safety
Ask Google for route options between two points, then score each against everything already coded across saved routes - not a fresh Pull Risk run, just what's already known. Coverage varies by road, so treat gaps as "not yet known," not "safe."
Field verification
Drop photos or video from a drone/GoPro flight - matched to the nearest point by GPS automatically. Fills points marked "No Street View coverage", and attaches as a separate validation image on points that already have coverage, so you can compare what was actually seen against the Street View baseline.
