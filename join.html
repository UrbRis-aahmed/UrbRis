UrbRis is a safety and training community for people who ride motorcycles, drive manual and fly. Membership is free. Once we reach 5,000 members, we can negotiate a group insurance discount for everyone.

  • MeetupsCoffee and BBQ meetups for people who ride, drive stick and fly.
  • A group insurance rateThe goal at 5,000 members. A discount depends on an insurer agreeing to offer it, so it isn't guaranteed.

Become a member

Takes 30 seconds. One membership per person.

UrbRis is a free, donation-supported community. Membership doesn't sign you up for insurance or share your details with any insurer unless you ask for the group rate. Any future group discount would be offered and underwritten by a licensed insurer under its own eligibility rules.
